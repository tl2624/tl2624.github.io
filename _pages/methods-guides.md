- **Difference-in-Differences website** (with Bret Zeldow and Laura Hatfield).  
  - [Difference-in-Differences](https://diff.healthpolicydatascience.org/)

- **Foundational proofs in design-based causal inference.** Standard expositions often compress these derivations; these guides show every step. The results concern the Difference-in-Means estimator and two estimands: the sample average treatment effect (SATE), which averages individual effects over the study units, and the population average treatment effect (PATE), which averages individual effects over the superpopulation from which the study draws its units.
  - [Notation and setup](https://tl2624.github.io/files/00_notation_and_setup.pdf)
  - [Unbiasedness of the Difference-in-Means estimator for the SATE and the PATE](https://tl2624.github.io/files/01_difference_in_means_unbiasedness.pdf)
  - [Variance of the Difference-in-Means estimator for the SATE and estimation of that variance](https://tl2624.github.io/files/02_difference_in_means_variance_sate.pdf)
  - [Variance of the Difference-in-Means estimator for the PATE and estimation of that variance](https://tl2624.github.io/files/03_difference_in_means_variance_pate.pdf)
