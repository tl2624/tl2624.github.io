I am an Assistant Professor (tenure track) at the Marxe School of Public and International Affairs, Baruch College, City University of New York (CUNY). My research develops advanced statistical methods for causal inference — tools for drawing credible conclusions about cause and effect from experimental and observational data — with applications to racial discrimination, policing, gun policy, and voter behavior. I am also the author and maintainer of open-source statistical software, including [apm](https://tl2624.github.io/apm/), the R package that accompanies my article on averaged prediction models for causal inference in controlled pre-post settings.

Before joining Baruch, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Harvard University (2021–2023), and I earned my Ph.D. in Political Science from Columbia University, specializing in methodology and comparative politics. I also serve as an Evaluation and Evidence Consulting Fellow with the Arnold Ventures Criminal Justice Evaluation Network. My work appears in *The Annals of Applied Statistics*, *International Statistical Review*, the *Journal of Causal Inference*, *Observational Studies*, *Political Analysis*, and *Political Science Research and Methods*. My [research](/research/) page collects my published and working papers, and my [curriculum vitae](/cv/) provides a complete record of my research, teaching, and service.

In addition to my regular teaching at the Marxe School, I have taught the Causal Inference course at the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR) summer program since 2018, and I taught a course on African politics at the City College of New York. My [teaching](/teaching/) page lists my courses, and my [methods guides](/methods-guides/) collect additional pedagogical materials. Before starting my Ph.D., I received an M.A. from the University of Chicago, a B.A. from DePauw University, and lived for two years in Uganda, where I served as a research assistant at Makerere University. I currently live in the Bronx, but was born and raised in Brooklyn, New York.
