I am an Assistant Professor (tenure track) at the Marxe School of Public and International Affairs, Baruch College, City University of New York (CUNY). My research develops advanced statistical methods for causal inference — tools for drawing credible conclusions about cause and effect from experimental and observational data — with applications to racial discrimination, policing, and gun policy. Much of my work draws on design-based inference, which bases causal conclusions on how a study assigns treatment rather than on modeling assumptions alone. Recent projects develop methods for matching, difference-in-differences, and sensitivity analysis, as well as approaches that unite design-based and Bayesian inference. I also build open-source statistical software, including [apm](https://tl2624.github.io/apm/), an R package for averaged prediction models.

Before joining Baruch, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Harvard Medical School (2021–2023), and I earned my Ph.D. in Political Science from Columbia University in 2021. I also serve as an Evaluation and Evidence Consulting Fellow with the Arnold Ventures Criminal Justice Evaluation Network. My work appears in *The Annals of Applied Statistics*, *International Statistical Review*, the *Journal of Causal Inference*, *Observational Studies*, *Political Analysis*, and *Political Science Research and Methods*. My [research](/research/) page collects my published and working papers, and my [curriculum vitae](/cv/) provides a complete record of my research, teaching, and service.

I have also served as an instructor for the Causal Inference course at the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR) summer program since 2018 and taught a course on African politics at the City College of New York (CCNY). Before starting my Ph.D., I received an M.A. from the University of Chicago, a B.A. from DePauw University, and lived for two years in Uganda, where I served as a research assistant at Makerere University. I currently live in the Bronx, but was born and raised in Brooklyn, New York.
